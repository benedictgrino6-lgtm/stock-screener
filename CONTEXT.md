# Who this brief is for

The `market-brief` agent reads this every day and writes for **this reader**. Edit it as
things change — the brief adapts automatically.

## Reader profile (as of 2026-09-09)

- **Options knowledge:** near zero. Does not yet know call vs put, strike, expiry, open
  interest, implied volatility. **Every term must be glossed in 3–6 plain words the first
  time it appears**, e.g. "open interest (how many contracts are currently held)".
- **Goal:** learn to read the market and understand how large players move. **Not trading.**
- **Account:** none. The brief is purely informational — it is teaching material, not
  advice.
- **Attention budget:** ~10 seconds. Wants a one-line verdict, then the option to read 2–3
  short items. Never a wall of text.

## What "prepared for" means for this reader

They are not placing trades, so "what to be prepared for" is a **learning prompt**, not a
setup: "watch <ticker> over the next few days — if it jumps, today's call buying was an
early hint; if nothing happens, that's the lesson that unusual flow often means nothing."

## Tone

Calm, plain, a little bit teacherly. No hype. No "smart money is loading up." When the
honest answer is "nothing today," say exactly that and stop.
